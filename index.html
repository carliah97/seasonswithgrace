Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you request a quote.

How do I get a quote?

Complete the quote form with your route, preferred date, pet information, and any special-care needs. We’ll review the request and contact you about availability and pricing.

Do you handle long-distance trips?

Long-distance and cross-state ground-transport requests can be submitted through the quote form. Availability depends on the requested route and schedule.

Can I request transport for more than one pet?

Yes. Enter the number of pets and explain each pet’s details or separate care needs in the notes.

Can I include medication or special-care instructions?

Yes. Include the details in your request. The requested care should be reviewed and confirmed before a booking is accepted.

What documents are needed?

Requirements can vary based on the pet, route, destination, and current regulations. Required documents should be confirmed before pickup.

What if weather affects the trip?

Safety comes first. Timing or routing may need to change when conditions make the original plan unsafe or impractical.
